# Pier Nine

At dawn, the camera glides just above a still fjord toward a small dark timber sauna floating on a pontoon. A single wide window faces the water. Pale morning light and the cabin’s reflection make the approach feel quiet and bright.

Inside, the camera eases toward the same window past warm timber benches. The fjord and dawn light carry through the cut, so the window that drew us toward the cabin now frames the view back out. The sauna is empty.
